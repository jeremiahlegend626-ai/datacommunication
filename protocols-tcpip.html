Protocols are sets of rules and standards that govern how data is transmitted, received, and processed in a network. They ensure compatibility and reliable communication.

TCP/IP Model

  • TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol): The fundamental protocol suite of the Internet, consisting of a 4-layer model:
    • Application Layer: HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SMTP, DNS
    • Transport Layer: TCP (reliable), UDP (fast)
    • Internet Layer: IP (addressing and routing)
    • Link Layer: Ethernet, PPP (physical transmission)

TCP ensures reliable, ordered delivery of data through error-checking and retransmission. UDP provides faster but unreliable delivery, suitable for streaming and real-time applications.
